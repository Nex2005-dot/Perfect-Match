Your information

Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Perfect Match uses information when you create an account or use photo analysis.

Information we collect

When you register, we collect the account details you provide, such as your name, username, and email address, along with your password in hashed form. If you use the camera, frames may be sent to our server for face detection. A selected image is processed in your browser to replace its background with white, then sent to our server for face detection and analysis.

How information is used and stored

We use this information to operate your account, analyze your photo, provide cosmetic recommendations, and display saved results. Analysis photos are not added to your account history unless you choose “Save Result.” Saved results and their photos remain in your history until you delete them or delete your account.

Background removal runs in your browser using a segmentation model loaded from a content delivery network. The image is not sent to that network by the background-removal step. Face detection and skin analysis use the Perfect Match server.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete individual saved analyses or clear your analysis history from your account. Deleting your account also deletes its associated analyses and photos. Guest analyses are not saved to account history.

Contact and related terms

For questions about your information, use the contact details provided by the project administrator. See our Terms and Conditions for the rules for using Perfect Match.
